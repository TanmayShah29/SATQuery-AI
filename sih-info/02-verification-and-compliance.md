# SIH 2026: Verification, Compliance & Documentation Checklist

Every Smart India Hackathon entry must pass rigid institutional and national compliance gates. Non-compliance at any check results in immediate administrative disqualification regardless of technical merit.

---

## 1. Core Eligibility Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Team Size**: Exactly **6 student members** (including the Team Leader). Neither 5 nor 7 members are accepted under any circumstances.
2. **Gender Mandate**: At least **one female member** is mandatory. Teams without at least one female member cannot register on the portal.
3. **Same Institute Rule**: All 6 members must be regular, enrolled students of the **same college or institution**. Inter-college teams are strictly forbidden. Members can, however, belong to different years, academic programs (B.Tech, M.Tech, BCA, MCA, etc.), or departments within the same institute.
4. **Mentors (Optional but Strongly Advised)**: Maximum 2 mentors per team (1 internal academic faculty mentor + 1 external industry mentor, or 2 faculty members). Mentors do not count towards the 6-student cap.
5. **Team Name Integrity**: The team name must be distinctive and must NOT contain the name of your college or university.

---

## 2. Verification Checklist for September 12 (Internal Round 1)

Prepare physical copies and a single consolidated PDF folder containing:

| Item | Requirement | Verification Target |
|---|---|---|
| **College Identity Cards** | Physical original + clear color scans of both sides for all 6 members. | Valid enrollment status, current academic year. |
| **Aadhaar / National ID** | Scanned PDF of all 6 members (needed for SIH portal registration). | Identity authentication. |
| **Bonafide / Semester Registration Slip** | Latest fee receipt or semester grade card confirming active enrollment. | Enrolled student verification. |
| **Team Leader Designation** | Single primary point of contact with active mobile number and official email. | Portal communication ownership. |
| **Problem Statement Confirmation** | PS Code (e.g. `SIH26001`), Title, Ministry/Organization, and Theme. | Match with active PS list on `sih.gov.in`. |
| **Slide Deck (Draft)** | Conforming to the 6-slide SIH template. | Presentation readiness. |

---

## 3. Verification Checklist for September 14 (Internal Round 2 / SPOC Nomination)

| Item | Requirement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Incorporated Jury Feedback** | Revised PPT with notes addressing critiques raised on Sept 12. | Proves team adaptability and responsiveness. |
| **Wireframe / Prototype Artifact** | Clickable Figma, basic working frontend, or architecture blueprint. | Differentiates team from presentation-only entries. |
| **SPOC Internal Endorsement Sheet** | Internal selection score sheet signed by the judging panel. | Internal college record. |
| **Draft College Authorization Letter** | Formatted letter ready for the Principal's / Dean's official signature and seal. | Required for portal upload. |
