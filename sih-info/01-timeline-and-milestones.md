---

```

   (Now – Sept 11)                   (Sept 12 & Sept 14)                   (Sept 15 – Sept 30)

        (December 2026)                    (November 2026)                     (October 2026)
```

---

## 2. Detailed Milestone Calendar

### Phase 1: Preparation & Team Lock (Current – Sept 11)
- **Objective**: Freeze team, finalize problem statement, create clickable prototype or architecture diagram, and build the internal round PPT.
- **Key Output**: 6-slide deck, system architecture diagram, team documentation dossier.

### Phase 2: College Internal Hackathon (Sept 12 & Sept 14)
- **Sept 12 (Round 1 / Preliminary Review & Document Verification)**:
  - Physical or offline/online internal presentation before college faculty panel / external judges.
  - Verification of team composition (6 members, mandatory 1 female, valid college IDs).
  - Presentation pitch: 5-minute presentation + 3-minute Q&A.
  - Evaluation of novelty, feasibility, and alignment with the chosen ministry problem statement.
- **Sept 14 (Round 2 / Final Shortlisting & SPOC Selection)**:
  - Demonstration of progress, prototype/wireframe walkthrough, or revised presentation incorporating Sept 12 feedback.
  - Official announcement of shortlisted teams nominated to represent the college.
  - Collection of signed college authorization / consent letter from Principal/Director.

### Phase 3: National Portal Idea Submission (Sept 15 – Sept 30)
- **Deadline**: September 30, 2026 (Official SIH 2026 Portal Idea Submission cutoff).
- **Responsible Party**: College SPOC (Single Point of Contact) and Team Leader.
- **Action Items**:
  - SPOC logs into `https://www.sih.gov.in/` using institutional credentials.
  - SPOC nominates shortlisted teams (up to 30–45 software + hardware teams per institute limit).
  - Upload official idea presentation PDF (converted from official SIH PPT format).
  - Upload college nomination/authorization letter signed by Head of Institute.

### Phase 4: National Evaluation & Screening (October 2026)
- Evaluators from ministries, departments, and industry partners review submitted idea decks remotely.
- Evaluation parameters: novelty (25%), technical feasibility (25%), impact/market potential (20%), user experience / architecture (15%), submission completeness (15%).
- Over 500+ ideas are submitted per popular problem statement; only 4 to 6 teams per problem statement qualify for the Grand Finale at a designated Nodal Center.

### Phase 5: Result Declaration & Nodal Center Allotment (November 2026)
- List of Grand Finale shortlisted teams published on `sih.gov.in`.
- Nodal Center (Host College/University/Institute across India) assigned to each problem statement.
- Teams receive confirmation emails and must confirm participation, travel logistics, and mentor assignments.

### Phase 6: Grand Finale (36-Hour Continuous Hackathon, December 2026)
- On-site, non-stop 36-hour physical hackathon at the assigned Nodal Center.
- 3 mentoring rounds and 3 jury evaluation rounds with ministry officials and industry experts.
- Live software deployment, hardware demonstration, and final trophy/cash prize announcement (₹1,00,000 per problem statement).
